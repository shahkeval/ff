CEDAR BLUFF COMMONS
Policies and Procedures Manual (Excerpt)
Last reviewed: 2026-08-15

SECTION 1 — ADMISSION AND RESIDENCY
1.1 Admission Criteria: Prospective residents are assessed by the Director of
Nursing or designee prior to admission to confirm the community can meet
their assessed care needs at the level requested (Independent Living,
Assisted Living, Memory Care, or Skilled Nursing).
1.2 Non-Discrimination: Admission decisions are made without regard to race,
color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, or source of payment,
consistent with the Fair Housing Act and applicable state regulation.
1.3 Resident Agreement: Every resident or responsible party signs a
residency agreement disclosing services included, fee schedule, and
transfer/discharge criteria prior to move-in.

SECTION 2 — MEDICATION MANAGEMENT
2.1 Administration: Medications are administered only by staff licensed or
certified to do so under state law (RN, LPN, or certified medication aide
where permitted).
2.2 Error Reporting: Any medication error is documented on an incident
report within the shift it occurs and reviewed by the DON within 24 hours.
2.3 Controlled Substances: Controlled substances are stored in a
double-locked cabinet or cart and counted at every shift change by two
staff members.

SECTION 3 — EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS
3.1 The community maintains a written emergency operations plan covering
fire, severe weather, power loss, and medical surge, reviewed annually.
3.2 Fire drills are conducted on each shift at least quarterly and
documented.
3.3 A 96-hour supply of food, water, and emergency medical supplies is
maintained per resident, per state licensing requirements.

SECTION 4 — INFECTION PREVENTION AND CONTROL
4.1 An Infection Preventionist is designated for the community and completes
required training within 90 days of assignment.
4.2 Standard precautions are followed for all resident care; transmission-
based precautions are added per CDC guidance when indicated.
4.3 Outbreaks of two or more cases of a reportable illness are reported to
the local health department within the timeframe required by state law.

SECTION 5 — RESIDENT RIGHTS AND GRIEVANCES
5.1 Every resident receives a written statement of rights at admission,
including the right to voice grievances without reprisal.
5.2 Grievances are logged, investigated, and a written response is provided
to the resident or responsible party within 30 calendar days.
5.3 Residents retain the right to participate in care planning and to
refuse treatment consistent with applicable law.

SECTION 6 — STAFFING AND TRAINING
6.1 Direct care staff complete orientation before their first unsupervised
shift, covering emergency procedures, resident rights, abuse reporting, and
infection control.
6.2 Annual mandatory training includes at minimum: fire/life safety, abuse
and neglect reporting, infection control, and HIPAA privacy.
6.3 Staffing levels are reviewed against the community's staffing model at
least monthly and adjusted for occupancy and acuity.

SECTION 7 — INCIDENT AND ACCIDENT REPORTING
7.1 All falls, injuries of unknown origin, and elopement attempts are
documented on an incident report the same day and reviewed at the weekly
risk management meeting.
7.2 Reportable incidents are submitted to the state licensing agency within
the timeframe the state requires.

SECTION 8 — DISCHARGE AND TRANSFER
8.1 Residents are given written notice of transfer or discharge, the reason,
and the effective date, except in cases of emergency transfer for the
resident's immediate health and safety.
8.2 A discharge summary and current medication list accompany every
resident transferred to a hospital or another care setting.

Approved by: Executive Director, Cedar Bluff Commons
