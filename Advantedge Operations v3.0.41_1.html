Getting started

Welcome.
Let's set up your community.

Over the next several screens, the tool will ask you to upload the reports and documents your community already produces — financial exports, operating-system reports from each department, residency and vendor contracts, policies and procedures, and any recent regulatory inspections. The analysis reviews everything against industry benchmarks, your own policies, your local labor and senior-living markets, and the performance goals you set — then surfaces specific opportunities to improve operational, financial, and clinical performance with a prioritized action plan.

How your data is handled
Documents are read only to produce your analysis. AI processing runs through Advantedge’s secured service — included with your subscription, no separate AI account or API key to manage. Your files are not used to train models.
Start here

First, line up your reports.

Pick the systems your community runs. We'll build the exact list of reports to pull — download it as an Excel file and hand it to your department heads so they can start running reports while you finish setup. You'll upload them in later steps.

Operating Systems & Reporting Guide

Choose the software your community uses for each department. We'll generate a checklist of exactly which reports to download — these supplement the financial uploads (COA, GL, P&L, Budget) so every analysis has the data it needs.

Running financials out of your EHR? Platforms like PointClickCare, MatrixCare, American HealthTech and Eldermark also appear under Finance / Accounting as a separate "— Financial / Billing" entry. Pick the clinical version under Nursing/Clinical for quality reports, and the Financial / Billing version under Finance for your GL, trial balance, AR and budget exports.
Why this is first
Reports usually live with department heads and take time to run. Getting the list into their hands now means the data is ready by the time you reach the upload steps — instead of stalling setup to chase exports later.

Account & Department Mapping

After your COA is parsed, detected accounts and departments appear above. Account-to-department mapping is finalized in the Advantedge Operations workspace, where your edits are saved and reused for every period going forward.

Why we ask for this first
Without the COA, every transaction's department assignment is an inference. With it, we have a deterministic mapping. The COA is reused for every period going forward — upload once, benefit forever.

If you operate a Skilled Nursing Facility
CMS requires every SNF to maintain a written QAPI Plan (Quality Assurance & Performance Improvement, 42 CFR §483.75) and an Emergency Preparedness Plan (42 CFR §483.73 — all-hazards approach, drill records, continuity of operations). Upload both above and tag them “QAPI” and “Emergency Prep” so we can cite them directly in your action plans and surface gaps against CMS’s required elements.
Non-SNF State Surveys
